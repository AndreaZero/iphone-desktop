Enable camera access on this iPhone

Safari needs a trusted HTTPS connection. Return to the setup link shown on your PC and download its local certificate.

  1. Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and install the downloaded iPhone Desktop Local Camera profile.
  2. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and enable full trust for iPhone Desktop Local Camera.
  3. Return to the secure camera link in Safari and reload this page.

Only install the certificate from your own PC. You can remove it from VPN & Device Management when you no longer use this camera.

Keep Safari visible and the iPhone unlocked. Locking the screen or switching apps stops the camera.
